================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING FOR USERS
================================================================================

Issue: "MongoDB not found"
Solution: Run setup.bat to download MongoDB

Issue: "Application won't start"
Solution: 
  1. Delete 'data' folder
  2. Restart plumbing-crm.exe
  3. Delete 'mongodb' folder and re-run setup.bat

Issue: "Port 5000 in use"
Solution: Change port in launcher.js (advanced users)

Issue: "Slow startup"
Solution: First startup may take longer (creating database)

Issue: "Data lost"
Solution: Restore from backup (see DATA & BACKUP section)

More help: See README.txt or START_HERE.txt
